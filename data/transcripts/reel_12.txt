If there's one study trick I learned at Harvard that changed my life, it's this one. Last year, I discovered that jotting down notes during lectures sometimes prevents me from actually comprehending concepts. So instead, go to turbolearn.ai during class and hit record. The software will automatically use your recording to make notes of flashcards and quiz questions. If you have questions about concepts, the chatbot will even help answer your questions based on your professor's lecture. This way, you don't have to worry about scrambling down information and you can instead focus 100% of your attention on understanding the lecture content.
