# Kiln

Kiln is made up for the demo. It is not a real company and not YC-backed.

## What it does (made up for the demo)
An AI study app that turns your lecture notes into flashcards and quizzes.

## Who it's for (made up for the demo)
University students.

## Tone (made up for the demo)
A friend in your program at 11pm before a midterm. Casual, specific, a bit tired, never salesy.

## Features (made up for the demo)
- Drop in typed notes, slides or a photo of handwriting, get a deck back
- Quizzes written like exam questions, not just definitions
- Cards you get wrong come back sooner
- Each answer points to the line in your notes it came from
- Cram mode: say when the exam is, it picks what to study first

## Student pain points (made up for the demo)
- Pages of notes you never open again
- Rereading feels like studying, then you blank in the exam
- Making the flashcards takes longer than studying them
- Three midterms in one week and no idea where to start
- You find out what you don't know during the exam

## Call to action (made up for the demo)
Drop tonight's lecture notes into Kiln and quiz yourself before bed.

## Never claim
- User counts, grade improvements, reviews, investors or school partnerships
- That the speaker really used Kiln or got a result from it
- A website, handle or app store listing
